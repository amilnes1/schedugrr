# Code of conduct

ScheduGrr welcomes thoughtful contributions from people of every background
and experience level. Be respectful, assume good faith, critique work rather
than people, and make space for others to participate.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate humiliation, and publication
of another person's private information are not acceptable in project spaces.
Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
conduct undermines a safe, constructive community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the hosting
provider's private contact or security-reporting facilities. Reports will be
handled as confidentially and promptly as practical.
